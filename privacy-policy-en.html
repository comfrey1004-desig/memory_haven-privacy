Memory Haven Privacy Policy

Memory Haven (기억건축소)

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Memory Haven (the "App") is a personal journal in which each memory you keep becomes a brick, and the bricks build houses and a village. This policy explains what information the App handles, where, and how.

Our privacy principle
The memories, photos and recordings you keep are stored only on your device. The developer of Memory Haven does not send them to or store them on any server, and the App has no sign-up, advertising or usage analytics.

1. Information the App handles

The App handles the following information that you enter or choose:

  • Memories: text, date and time, and the feelings you select
  • Photos you choose
  • Sounds you record yourself
  • House details: name, blueprint, place in the village and decoration
  • App settings: display language, whether the first-run guide was completed, and Story playback settings

This information is used only to keep your memories, show your houses and village, and play your memory Stories.

2. Where it is stored

All of the above is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access.

The developer of Memory Haven runs no server that receives or keeps your memories, photos or recordings.

3. Photos

The App uses the Android system photo picker and handles only the photos you pick. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

A picked photo is copied into the App's private storage so it can be attached to a memory. Your original photo is never changed or deleted.

4. Microphone and recordings

The App asks for and uses the microphone only when you start sound recording or voice typing. You can deny or withdraw this permission at any time in Android settings and still keep memories with text and photos.

Recorded sounds are attached to the memory and stored only in the App's private storage on your device.

5. Voice typing (dictation)

Voice typing uses the Android speech recognition service installed on your device (for example, Google speech recognition) to turn speech into text. While you use it, your speech is passed to that service and may be processed over the network under the service provider's policy.

The App receives only the resulting text and places it in the memory you are writing. The developer of Memory Haven does not receive or store your speech.

If you do not use voice typing, no speech is passed to a speech recognition service.

6. Backup and restore

When you start a backup, the App packs your memories, houses, photos and sounds into one file, encrypts it with a password you choose, and saves it where you choose. The backup password is not stored in the App.

You decide where the backup file is kept. If you choose a cloud storage service, that provider's policy applies.

The App is set so that Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer do not copy its data.

7. Internet use

The App holds the internet permission only for voice typing. It does not otherwise send your information over the internet.

8. Accounts, advertising and analytics

The App has no sign-up or log-in. It uses no advertising SDK and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

9. Sale and sharing of personal information

The developer of Memory Haven does not sell your information or share it with third parties. While you use voice typing (section 5), your speech is passed to your device's speech recognition service.

10. Retention and deletion

Information stays on your device until you delete it.

  • You can delete each memory in the App.
  • Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in Android settings, removes everything the App stored from your device.
  • Backup files you made remain where you saved them, and you can delete them there.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not designed to collect children's personal information, and the developer collects no user's information.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may change with the App's features or with applicable laws and policies. Any change and its effective date will be posted on this page.

13. Contact

For questions about privacy or this policy, please use the developer contact listed on Google Play.
